| id | choice | discarded alternative | where it enters |

No other choice is used. In particular the field ℚ(√−3), the label E6, the Bianchi group and the 14-member family are
consequences of A1–A3 (T02, T05), not choices; and nothing in T05 is a property of the object rather than of its class.
